> > 2.2.2. Scaling Scaling generally refers to measurements or assessments conducted under exact, specified and repeatable conditions. In ML, scaling transforms feature values according to a defined rule so that all scaled features have the same degree of influence [36] and thus the method is immune to the choice of units [71], which is a major stage for ML methods. Normally, the intervals of [0, 1] and [1, 1] are used to be the target of scaling, as shown in Eq. (1).
